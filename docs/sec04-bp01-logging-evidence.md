session-specific streams demonstrates that CloudWatch Logs

of application-level event coverage or the contents of the logs.

## 5. Limitations and residual risks

- Retention is configured as Never expire; an approved retention
  period has not been documented.
- Deletion protection is disabled.
- CloudWatch data protection is not configured.
- No metric filters or subscription filters were observed.
- Log access permissions have not been audited.
- The contents of the streams were not inspected for sensitive data.
- Only the us-east-1 DepGuard Runtime log group was examined.
- No automated alerting or log-processing workflow was validated.

The absence of a customer-managed KMS key does not establish
that CloudWatch Logs data is unencrypted; encryption configuration
requires separate verification.
